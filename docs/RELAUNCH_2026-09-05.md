## Tier D Level-clear item rewards — 2026-09-14

- Bronze, Silver and Gold (Lv.1–150): one random item; a verified rewarded ad
  adds one more copy (x2 total for that reward).
- Platinum, Diamond and Elite (Lv.151–350): two distinct random items, one
  each; a verified ad adds one further random item.
- Master, Grandmaster and Legend (Lv.351–1000): two distinct random item types,
  each with a random quantity of one or two; a verified ad adds two more items.
- Mythic and later (Lv.1001+): three distinct random items; a verified ad adds
  two more items. Existing VIP/No-Ads entitlement receives that League's ad
  bonus automatically without an ad.
- Migration 062 expands the Level reward ledger into idempotent reward slots;
  the Server owns the band, randomized item selection, quantities and ad claim.
- A cleared Level stops at `CLAIM REWARD`; the rolled items are shown only after
  that explicit claim. VIP receives its automatic bonus there and can then
  continue to the next Level.
- To keep the item economy healthy, ordinary clears no longer grant an item.
  A Level reward is reserved only for a new all-time Longest Streak, a new
  all-time fastest Level clear, or a Level ending in zero. The criteria are
  evaluated by the server before it records the new statistics.
- Persistent inventory is always merged with temporary Match grants in the
  Tier D item rail. Stored Undo is no longer level-gated; it is available
  whenever the current revealed pile is still eligible to undo.

## Launch decisions

- `/` and the old lobby entry now lead to `/launch`, outside authentication.
- `/duel` is a complete on-device solo game against one clearly identified AI
  rival. One rival keeps the nine scoring contests readable for beginners.
- Three hands per match; each hand deals 11 private cards to each player plus
  three pairs of shared cards, all from one shuffled 52-card deck.
- Arrange 3 / 3 / 5 private cards. Evaluate exactly three private cards plus the
  corresponding two shared cards; Pile 3 chooses its best three automatically.
- Require full poker strength Pile 1 <= Pile 2 <= Pile 3, including kickers.
- Each pile win scores 1, each tie scores 0.5 per player. Most total points wins;
  tied matches remain draws, with no arbitrary tie-break or forced overtime.
- Each player has one seal per match. Commit it to a pile before locking. An
  outright win adds 1 point; losses and ties consume it without a bonus. It may
  be saved across hands and expires unused at match end.
- The rival chooses its seal hand before dealing and arranges only its own cards
  and shared cards. No reading player cards, hidden catch-up, or loss balancing.
- Players reveal each pile at their own pace. The visible score includes only
  revealed piles. No countdown, entry fee, ad gate, wallet loss, or daily obligation.
- Legal starter arrangements, three free strategic suggestions, manual tap-to-swap,
  inline foul prevention, and a rules panel support learning.
- Results show each hand and seal outcome. Replay lets players change their own
  arrangement against the same revealed rival plan and original seals. It is
  explicitly hindsight practice, never another scored match or a reward source.

## Persistence and advanced access

One versioned AsyncStorage record holds the active match and local progression.
Drafts, seal selection, locked hands, reveal position and results survive restart.
Writes are serialized. Match completion is idempotent, and is counted when the
player finishes all reveals and opens the result screen. Save & exit pauses the
session; starting a fresh match is offered only on completed results.

Advanced access requires 5 completed matches, 2 seal wins and a four-question
learning check. These are provisional learning thresholds, not calendar gates.
The check explains ordering, shared cards, seal ties and the separate old rules.
Progress is explicitly device-local: it is not an account achievement, paid
entitlement, server security boundary, leaderboard, or a source of tokens.

The original lobby lives at `client/app/(home)/classic-lobby.tsx`. Its old
authentication, token eligibility and server-side authority remain in force.
Home and game route guards prevent accidental early entry into advanced tables.
The previous Grandmaster development preview exception is retained. Advanced
ad-return URLs point to the preserved lobby. Old match exit links return to the
new launch hub. Existing server engines and wallets are untouched.
